What to clarify for a Yaba brief

For a photo, podcast or video request, name the room or venue, recording window, deliverable formats, equipment, speakers or talent and revision route. For a music or sync request, list the track, rights holders, usage, territory, term and media channels.

For a local business, separate the website or social content task from GBP management and from paid advertising. A manager, editor, producer and ad operator may be different roles even when one person coordinates the project.
