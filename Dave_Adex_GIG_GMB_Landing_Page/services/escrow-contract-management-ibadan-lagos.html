Why clients use Escrow & Contract Management in Ibadan & Lagos

Projects across Ibadan, Lagos and remote markets can become difficult when scope, deadlines and payment expectations are only discussed in chat. This service adds a documented structure so the buyer and talent understand what is being delivered and when.

What this service includes

Dave Adex helps organise the work-for-hire terms, milestones, acceptance points and communication responsibilities. Where an escrow arrangement is used, the process and broker fee are agreed in writing; the agency does not hide the conditions for releasing funds.

  • ✓Work scope and deliverables checklist
  • ✓Contract and milestone coordination
  • ✓Payment-release process agreed by the parties

What to expect from Dave Adex

The service is designed to reduce avoidable misunderstandings, not to replace legal advice or a regulated payment provider. Each project is reviewed for scope, parties and risk before a contract-management approach is recommended.
