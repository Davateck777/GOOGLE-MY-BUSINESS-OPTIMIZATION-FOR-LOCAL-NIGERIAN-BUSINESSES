## Editorial model

The content is not a city-name swap. Each guide is built around a real decision a client or creative professional needs to make: briefing talent, planning event staffing, defining usage, checking GBP eligibility, separating setup from monthly management, or responding to reputation pressure.

The FAQ blocks stay brief on Core pages and link to deeper guides so the main service pages do not become walls of text.

## Research and reference trail

Questions were reworded rather than copied. Research themes were checked against current search results and industry discussions around:

- Google Business Profile eligibility, identity, service areas and reviews
- Nigerian model casting and model-development FAQs
- Nigerian event ushering and event-staffing questions
- Music sync licensing, ownership and delivery questions
- Meta Business Suite message-management workflows

Reference links visible on the guide pages are used as context, not as endorsements or claims about Dave Adex. Prices, reviews, ratings, locations and partnerships were not invented.

## Important limitations

- The site has 28 Core pages, not 30, because it contains 24 supplied services plus 3 hubs and 1 homepage.
- A live Google People Also Ask capture and Reddit data export were not available as a workspace asset; prompts were reworded from observed search/industry themes rather than copied verbatim.
- A production domain and external backlink campaign are still required before measuring organic visibility or building inbound authority links.
- The sitemap uses relative paths until a production domain is supplied.
