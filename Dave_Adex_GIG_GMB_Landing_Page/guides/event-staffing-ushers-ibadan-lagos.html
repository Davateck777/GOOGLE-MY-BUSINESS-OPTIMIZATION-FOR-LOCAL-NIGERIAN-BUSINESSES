How to Plan Event Staffing and Ushers in Ibadan & Lagos

Plan event staffing around guest flow, duties, call time and venue conditions instead of choosing a headcount in isolation.

The right event staffing brief is about guest movement and responsibility, not only how many people can be placed at the entrance. In Ibadan and Lagos, the venue, programme, transport, guest profile and supplier layout all affect the team that should be dispatched.

Short answer

Start with expected guests, entrances, programme flow, staff duties, dress, call time, venue layout, transport and the event lead’s contact. Then decide whether you need ushers, brand ambassadors, stage support or a broader on-site crew.

Map the guest journey

Write down how a guest moves from arrival to departure: parking or drop-off, registration, security, seating, exhibition points, refreshments, bathrooms and exit. Mark the places where a guest is likely to ask for help. That map is more useful than a generic request for “beautiful ushers.”

A corporate launch may need people who can explain a product or guide media guests. A conference may need registration and room-direction support. A wedding or social event may need welcoming, seating and programme coordination. Match the role to the flow.

Give the team a working brief

Confirm venue, date, call time, hours, dress or uniform, language, guest profile, transport, meals, breaks, equipment and the person who can make decisions on site. Make clear what ushers should not do, especially where serving, security, money handling or personal requests are outside the agreed role.

For Lagos events, build realistic arrival time into the plan. For Ibadan events, clarify whether the team is local or travelling in. A well-briefed team can still encounter venue changes, so the event lead should have a simple escalation route.

Price the scope honestly

A starting guide for event-staffing work changes with the role, hours, number of people, training, wardrobe, transport and supervision. Dave Adex’s current service guide is shown on the dedicated event-staffing page; the final scope is confirmed after the brief.

Do not promise that staffing alone will solve every event problem. If the project needs stage management, technical crew, security or vendor coordination, name those responsibilities separately and price them as a separate scope where appropriate.

Brief checklist

  • ✓Expected guest volume and entry points
  • ✓Duties by role
  • ✓Venue, date, call time and hours
  • ✓Dress, training and transport
  • ✓Event lead and escalation contact

Questions this guide answers

How should a client estimate the number of ushers needed?

Begin with guest volume, entry points, seating or registration tasks, programme transitions and the level of supervision required; there is no safe headcount without the venue brief.

What should event staff know before arriving?

They need the call time, venue entrance, dress code, duties, escalation contact, guest-handling boundaries, breaks and transport expectations.

What changes between a conference, launch and private celebration?

The same staff title can hide different duties: registration, wayfinding, product demonstration, backstage support or guest care should be named separately.
