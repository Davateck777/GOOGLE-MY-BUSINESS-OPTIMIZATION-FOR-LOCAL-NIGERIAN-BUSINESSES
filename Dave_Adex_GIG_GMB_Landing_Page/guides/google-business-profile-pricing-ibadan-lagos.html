Google Business Profile Setup and Monthly Management Pricing in Ibadan & Lagos

Separate one-time profile setup from ongoing management so a local business knows what it is paying for.

A local business should be able to tell the difference between fixing or preparing a profile once and maintaining a workflow every month. This guide keeps the approved Dave Adex pricing visible while making clear that final scope depends on the business and its assets.

Short answer

The approved starting guide is ₦25,000 for Starter setup, previously ₦50,000, and ₦15,000/month for Monthly management, previously ₦30,000/month. Confirm current availability and scope before payment.

Approved starting prices

Starter setup: ₦25,000, previously ₦50,000. This is a one-time starting guide for preparing or aligning the profile around the approved business identity and scope.

Monthly management: ₦15,000/month, previously ₦30,000/month. This is an ongoing starting guide for agreed profile-maintenance and content tasks. Confirm what is included before work begins.

What setup and management should not promise

A setup fee does not guarantee verification, a specific Maps position, a particular number of calls or a review outcome. Google controls its platform and each business has different eligibility, competition and customer behaviour.

Monthly work should have a clear approval route. The business remains responsible for the accuracy of its identity, address, services, hours and claims. Dave Adex can coordinate the workflow but should not publish unsupported information on the client’s behalf.

Prepare the handoff before paying for activity

Gather the approved name, address or service area, phone, hours, services, photos, logo, review policy, owner access and decision-maker contact. A missing asset can delay a setup or make a monthly workflow less useful.

For Ibadan and Lagos, decide whether the business genuinely serves customers at the listed address, travels to customers, or needs a hybrid setup. That decision affects how the profile should be reviewed.

Brief checklist

  • ✓Approved business identity
  • ✓Accurate address or service area
  • ✓Phone, hours and services
  • ✓Owner access and approval contact
  • ✓Genuine photos and review process

Questions this guide answers

What does GBP setup normally cover?

A setup scope can include identity and NAP review, category and service alignment, profile-field preparation, ownership coordination and a practical handoff checklist.

Is monthly management the same as one-time setup?

No. Monthly management is an ongoing workflow for approved updates, photos, posts, review-response support and reporting; it is not a promise of a ranking position.

What should a client prepare before the work starts?

Prepare the exact business name, address or service area, phone, hours, logo, genuine photos, service list, owner access and approval contact.
