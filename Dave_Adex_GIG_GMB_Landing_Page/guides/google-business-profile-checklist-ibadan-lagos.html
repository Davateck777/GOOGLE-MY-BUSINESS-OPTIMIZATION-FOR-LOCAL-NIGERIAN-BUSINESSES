Google Business Profile Checklist for Businesses in Ibadan & Lagos

Keep the profile accurate, eligible and useful before adding more content or chasing rankings.

A Google Business Profile should make it easy for a real customer to understand who the business is, where it operates and how to contact it. For a business serving Ibadan and Lagos, accuracy matters more than stuffing both city names into every field.

Short answer

Confirm the real-world name, eligible address or service area, primary category, phone, hours, services, photos and ownership before adding posts or asking for reviews.

Start with eligibility and identity

Use the exact business name that appears in the real world. The primary category should describe what the business is, not become a long list of every service offered. Additional services belong in the appropriate profile fields and on the website.

For Dave Adex, the approved identity is Dave Adex GIG & GMB Agency and the primary category is Talent Agency. The website keeps the exact address and phone supplied for the current GBP identity, while clearly labeling the generated storefront image as a concept visual.

Decide whether the profile is storefront, service-area or hybrid

A customer-facing storefront needs to be genuinely staffed and able to receive customers during stated hours. A service-area business may operate from a base and travel to customers, but it must follow Google’s eligibility and address-display rules.

Serving Ibadan and Lagos in website copy does not automatically create a second eligible GBP location. The profile structure should reflect how the business actually meets customers and delivers work.

Complete the useful fields first

Check the primary category, services, phone, hours, website, service areas, photos and ownership access. Add genuine work or team images where available. Generated visuals can support the website and pitch materials, but genuine photos should be used for GBP uploads when they are meant to represent the business or its work.

Review responses should be honest and specific. Do not invent testimonials, offer incentives for reviews or promise a ranking position. Dave Adex’s website uses an honest review CTA rather than fabricated review content.

Brief checklist

  • ✓Eligibility and real-world identity
  • ✓Primary category and services
  • ✓Accurate address or service area
  • ✓Phone, hours and ownership access
  • ✓Genuine photos and honest review workflow

Questions this guide answers

What should a Nigerian business check before publishing a Google Business Profile?

Check eligibility, exact business name, accurate address or service area, primary category, phone, hours, services and genuine photos.

Can one profile serve both Ibadan and Lagos?

It depends on the actual business model, customer-facing location and service-area eligibility; do not create duplicate profiles simply to target another city.

What if the listed address is not a customer-facing storefront?

Review the storefront versus service-area rules carefully. A concept visual or mailing address should not be presented as proof of a staffed customer location.

Reference link

Google’s own guidelines should be checked before changing eligibility, address, category or service-area information.
