What to Put in a Commercial Model Casting Brief in Nigeria

Clarify look, role, shoot conditions and usage before asking models to audition or hold a date.

Commercial casting is easier to manage when the client describes the work precisely. A model may be right for a fashion image but wrong for a product demonstration, a multilingual social video or a long event activation. A clear brief protects time for the brand, production team and model.

Short answer

Describe the audience, campaign idea, model role, look direction, shoot date, hours, location, wardrobe, language or performance requirements, usage channels and budget before the shortlist is presented.

Start with the campaign, not a generic “model needed” post

A casting brief should tell the model and agency what the audience should understand or feel. Include whether the campaign is for a product launch, retail display, fashion story, corporate brand, event activation or online video.

Then describe the role: posing, presenting, demonstrating a product, greeting guests, speaking a line, walking a runway or appearing in a group. The more specific the role, the more useful the shortlist.

Record the production conditions

Add the shoot or event date, call time, expected hours, location, wardrobe, grooming, language, physical activity and any travel. If the client has a mood board, product references or sample frames, attach them to the brief rather than relying on adjectives such as “classy” or “bold.”

For Lagos projects, travel and timing can materially affect availability. For Ibadan projects, state whether the client needs talent already nearby or is willing to cover transport. Those details should be agreed before a hold becomes a booking.

Treat usage as part of the booking

Usage means where and how the finished images or video may appear. State the channels, territory, duration, paid-media use, exclusivity and whether the brand can edit the material into additional formats. A model rate cannot be judged properly without this context.

Dave Adex can help coordinate the brief, shortlist and booking conversation. The client and model should approve the final usage terms before the shoot, and neither a portfolio nor a casting request should be treated as proof of a guaranteed booking.

Brief checklist

  • ✓Campaign and audience
  • ✓Model role and performance requirement
  • ✓Look, wardrobe and grooming direction
  • ✓Shoot or event date, hours and location
  • ✓Media, territory, duration and exclusivity

Questions this guide answers

Which details belong in a commercial model casting brief?

State the campaign purpose, audience, look direction, role, date, location, hours, wardrobe, deliverables and usage channels.

How do usage rights change a model booking?

A short social post, a national advert and a long-term billboard campaign create different commercial scopes; confirm territory, duration and media before approval.

Does casting in Ibadan follow the same process as casting in Lagos?

The brief structure is similar, but the available pool, travel, venue access and production logistics may change the shortlist and schedule.
